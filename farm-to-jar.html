Farm to jar

Ever wondered how a jar of Batch 12 gets made? Here's a peek inside our kitchen, from fresh produce to sealed and labeled jars.

1. Prep the harvest2. Cook low and slow3. Fill every jar4. Sealed, labeled and ready
1

Prep the harvest

Farm-fresh produce gets washed, cut, roasted and peeled by hand.

2

Cook low and slow

Small batches on the stove, stirred until the texture is just right.

You can’t rush epic flavor

Our 24-Hour Apple Butter simmers low and slow for a full day so the apples’ natural sugars caramelize.

3

Fill every jar

Jars are filled one at a time with our trusty blue funnel.

4

Sealed, labeled and ready

Every finished jar gets a batch number, so you know exactly which kitchen day it came from.
